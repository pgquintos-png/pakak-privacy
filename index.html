Pakak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Pakak is a learning game for kids. We respect your family's privacy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Pakak has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, such as levels and stars earned, is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, Pakak is safe for children of all ages and complies with COPPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/pgquintos-png/pakak-privacy/issues.
